# The `/go` dead end wears the notice frame, and the route guard learned to see type drift

`BookingLinkNotFound` in `src/routes/go.tsx` now renders through `NoticePage tone="problem"` with `STANDALONE_LINK_CLASSES`, exactly as `ProfileNotFound` does. Its copy is unchanged and it keeps the `go-not-found` marker the E2E suite navigates by. The seventh screen joins the six ADR-0018 named.

**It was missed because the happy path never renders it.** `/go` is a loader that either 302s to the Practitioner's Booking Link or throws `notFound()`, so Slice 32 converted the four consumer routes that render something and left this one behind in stock Tailwind — `text-2xl`, `font-bold`, a bare `underline`. The page is rare but not hypothetical: a consumer arriving from a bookmark or a shared link to a Practitioner who has stopped being visible lands here, one hop after a page in MiCare's typeface, and reads the bad news in Tailwind's.

**The palette guard could not have caught it, so a second guard now runs beside it.** `stockPaletteUtilities` looks for `bg-black` and `text-gray-700`; this page reached past the tokens for a *size*, not a colour, and passed the guard clean. `stockTypeScaleUtilities` closes that: `text-xs`, `text-sm`, `text-xl` and the `text-2xl`–`text-9xl` ramp are drift, because MiCare names its sizes for the job they do — `text-meta`, `text-title`, `text-h1`. The two names that collide, `text-base` and `text-lg`, are redefined in the `@theme` block, so those two are ours and the guard leaves them alone. Font *weights* are not guarded: `font-bold` is Tailwind's and MiCare's alike, and there is no token that replaces it.

**`framesNotices` is a guard, not a rule about routes.** A route declares it when what it renders is the end of a journey rather than a step in one, and the guard then asserts the source composes `NoticePage` instead of a hand-built copy of it. It says nothing about routes that do not declare it — `/search` frames nothing and never should. Like the other two, it reads the source rather than the render, for the reason ADR-0018 gave: a TanStack file route cannot be mounted without a router, and the property being kept is a property of the file.
