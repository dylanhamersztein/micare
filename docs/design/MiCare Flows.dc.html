17 — Signup · the verification wait

Show the work while it happens.

Four or five seconds is long enough for a practitioner to wonder whether the form submitted. So the wait is not a spinner over a greyed page — it is a three-line record of what is being done, in the order it is being done, naming the register and the number. The whole proposition is “we checked”; this is the one screen where the user watches us check.

One thing moves on the page. Under prefers-reduced-motion the ring stops spinning and holds as a two-thirds arc — the state is already carried by the word “checking”, so nothing is lost.
