09 — Wordmark

A weight change, not a logo.

The wordmark is one word of Newsreader with a weight break: Mi at 600, Care at 400, −0.01em tracking. No symbol, no tick, no eye. The company that verifies other people's credentials should not be the one with a swoosh — and a purely typographic mark survives being rendered at 24px on a five-year-old Android at 1× DPI.

11 — Header · 390px

No drawer, no hamburger, no bottom bar.

There are three consumer destinations on this site and one of them is the page you are on. A hamburger would hide two links behind a gesture, cost a tap, and put a 24px icon in front of a user who came here because their eyes are not what they were. So: a two-row wrapped header. Row one is identity and account, 56px. Row two is the search control, full width, 56px — the only thing a consumer needs, permanently under their thumb.

“List your Practice” is not in the mobile header. Practitioners arrive from the homepage pitch or a link, deliberately, usually on a desktop with a card to hand — they do not need it on every consumer screen. It lives in the footer and in the homepage section, at full size.
